# New shared-finance ideas

Splitting bills is solved (Splitwise, Tricount, Revolut, Monzo). These five aren't shipped anywhere we could find.

1. **Payday Settle.** "Pay back from my next salary": when your salary lands, your debts to friends settle automatically by instant payment. Only the bank sees the salary arrive.

2. **Blind Budget.** Before a trip or dinner, everyone privately enters what they can afford; the group sees only "everyone's fine up to €280", never who set the low number. Works for groups of 3+.

3. **Rent Pact, bank covers the gap.** The landlord always gets full rent on the 1st. If a flatmate is short, the bank fronts their share and collects it from their next income. The bank chases, not you.

4. **Two-Key Spending.** On joint money, big purchases need one tap from the partner: "€400 bike?" → OK → card unlocked for 24 h. Monzo users have asked for it; nobody has built it.

5. **All-or-Nothing Trip Pot.** Everyone pledges their share and no money moves until all are in. Then the bank issues one virtual card for the booking. If someone drops out, everyone is released.

**Pick:** keep Spolu's receipt scan to open the demo, make Payday Settle the main feature, and use Blind Budget as the fake door.
